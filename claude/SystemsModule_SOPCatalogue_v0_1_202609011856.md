# Systems Module, SOP Catalogue

**Version:** 0.1, draft for approval
**Created:** 1 September 2026, 18:56 AEST
**Source:** The Calm Venue, chapters 3 to 24. Every core item is the chapter's own "habit to install", so the catalogue is the book, not a template library.
**Status:** For Julian to cut, rename and approve. Nothing is built from this until it is signed off.

## How this works

The operator sees a checklist and ticks two things per item: **do not have one**, and **have one but it is not working**. Both are diagnostic. A "not working" tick becomes a candidate breakpoint on the fragility map. The venue's two weakest Calm Index domains float to the top and arrive pre-suggested.

Ticking generates a starting procedure in cue, routine, reinforcement and owner form, then the module interviews the operator to fit it to their venue. Everything generated lands as `draft` and still has to pass the three method questions and the rostered-off gate before it counts as installed.

If more than four are ticked, the module does not refuse. It says the minimum system set is three or four, asks which three to install first, and starts only those as live. The rest sit in the library.

## Part B. Venue type variation

The seven domains do not change. What changes is which items lead, and a small number of type-specific additions. Proposal, for your cut:

| Type | Leads with | Type-specific additions |
|---|---|---|
| Cafe | A2 prep and roster, A5 the wait, A13 opening the room | Morning rush changeover; takeaway and dine-in split at the counter |
| Restaurant | A1 handover, A15 bill and goodbye, A16 recovery | Sitting changeover and table turn; the booking no-show call |
| Bar | A6 margin defaults, A13 signals, A16 recovery | Responsible service refusal, as an operational default rather than the legal obligation; last drinks and close-down |
| Pub | A1 handover, A6 margin, A18 compliance | Multi-area handover, bar to bistro to gaming; security handover |
| Hotel food and beverage | A1 handover, A3 weekly shape, A20 knowledge | Breakfast to lunch to dinner transitions; the interface with front office |
| Large format | A3 weekly shape, A9 fairness, A20 knowledge | Section-lead handover; how a standard travels across a big team |

Keep the additions to two per type. The moment it becomes a per-type template library, the method is gone and we are selling paperwork.

## Part C. Outside our remit

These are the SOPs a venue needs that the method has nothing to say about. The module does not author them and does not pretend to. It lists the headings, asks whether one exists, and where the answer is no, names where the recognised standard comes from and suggests the operator obtain it.

**How this is worded matters.** The module says "you appear to have no procedure covering this, here is the authority that sets the standard". It never says "here is your policy". Payroll, employment and safety carry legal exposure, and drafting those is a lawyer's or an accountant's job, not ours.

| Heading | Where the standard comes from |
|---|---|
| Pay rates, penalties and overtime | Fair Work Ombudsman, the applicable Award |
| Employment contracts and onboarding | Fair Work Ombudsman |
| Leave, absence and termination | Fair Work Ombudsman |
| Superannuation | Australian Taxation Office |
| Work health and safety | Safe Work Australia and the state regulator |
| Workers compensation and injury management | The state insurer |
| Food safety program and allergen control | FSANZ and the local council or state health department |
| Liquor licensing and responsible service | The state liquor authority |
| Fire, emergency and evacuation | The state regulator and the building's fire order |
| First aid | Safe Work Australia code of practice |
| Cash handling and security | Insurer requirements and the state regulator |
| Card payment handling | PCI DSS via the payment provider |
| Privacy and customer data | Office of the Australian Information Commissioner |
| Insurance and renewals | The venue's broker |
| Tax, BAS and record keeping | Australian Taxation Office |
| Waste, recycling and grease trap | Local council and the water authority |

Where a venue ticks "we do not have one", the module records it as an open obligation, shows it on the Systems screen as outside the audit, and leaves it there until the operator marks it sourced. It never counts toward the minimum system set and it never gets a verdict, because the three method questions do not apply to a payroll policy.
